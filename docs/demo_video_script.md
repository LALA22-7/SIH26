# CycloneWatch: 2-Minute Demonstration Video Script

**Target Duration:** ~2 minutes (approx. 260-300 words)
**Pacing:** Conversational, authoritative, and steady. Allow pauses for UI actions to speak for themselves.

---

### **0:00 - 0:20 | Introduction: The Problem**
**Visual:** Start on a dark screen or a title slide, then fade into a wide view of the CycloneWatch Dashboard (Live Map View).
**Voiceover (VO):** 
"Every year, tropical cyclones threaten millions of lives. While traditional physics-based weather models are incredibly accurate at predicting standard storm paths, they have a fatal flaw: they struggle to predict *Rapid Intensification*—when a storm explodes in strength almost overnight. By the time the models catch up, it's often too late. That interpretation gap costs lives."

### **0:20 - 0:45 | Introducing CycloneWatch & Live Dashboard**
**Visual:** Cursor moves around the dashboard. Show the deep ocean blue map, the glass-chrome panels, and the lemon-yellow accents. Toggle some layers (Base, Trajectory).
**VO:** 
"Enter CycloneWatch: an AI-powered tracking system designed to close that gap. What you are looking at is our real-time dashboard. Instead of waiting hours for physics simulations to run, our system feeds live satellite imagery directly into a deep learning model. In milliseconds, it analyzes the storm's structure, tracking it dynamically across the map."

### **0:45 - 1:25 | Historical Replay & The Core Value (Ockhi Case Study)**
**Visual:** Cursor clicks on "Historical Replay" in the left SideNav. Selects "Cyclone Ockhi (2017)". Cursor moves down to the Conveyor Belt Timeline at the bottom. Clicks and holds the right chevron to scrub forward through the satellite frames.
**VO:** 
"To understand the power of CycloneWatch, let's look at Cyclone Ockhi from 2017. Traditional models missed this storm's rapid intensification by 36 hours. 
Watch our historical timeline scrubber at the bottom. As we scrub forward, our AI is analyzing each satellite frame instantly. Look at the metrics panel on the right—long before traditional models issued a warning, our AI detects the structural transition and locks in the 'Rapid Intensification' alert."

### **1:25 - 1:50 | Deep Dive into Metrics**
**Visual:** Zoom in slightly on the right-side Metrics Panel. Highlight the AI Classification, Coast Distance, and Time to Impact counting down as the timeline moves.
**VO:** 
"Our AI doesn't just classify the pattern. It calculates critical, dynamic metrics. As the storm moves, you can see the exact distance to the Indian coastline updating in real-time, along with a dynamic 'Time to Impact'. Our forecast cone automatically scales based on the model's uncertainty, giving disaster management teams precise, actionable intelligence."

### **1:50 - 2:00 | Conclusion**
**Visual:** Zoom out to show the full dashboard one last time. Fade to the CycloneWatch Logo with the "AI-Powered Tracker" subscript. 
**VO:** 
"CycloneWatch isn't here to replace traditional meteorology. It’s here to act as an early warning system when every second counts. AI that doesn't just predict the weather—it beats the storm."

---

### **Shooting Tips for the Presenter:**
1. **Rehearse the UI clicks:** Make sure the cursor movements match the script perfectly. When the VO says "Watch our historical timeline", your mouse should already be interacting with it.
2. **Smooth Scrolling:** Use the new hold-to-scroll feature on the timeline buttons to make the satellite frames animate smoothly like a video, rather than clicking frantically.
3. **Pacing:** Don't rush the reading. The visual of the "Rapid Intensification" badge popping up on the right panel is the climax of the video—let it land.
